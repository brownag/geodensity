Replacement wording (copied from `R/geodensity.R` lines 153-158, `kde_adaptive` roxygen):

> The algorithm uses a two-pass approach: (1) compute the pilot density with the fixed `pilot_bandwidth`; (2) scale the bandwidth at each grid cell as `bw_i = min + (pilot - min) / (1 + sqrt(d_i / d_max))`, where `d_i` is the pilot density at the cell and `d_max` is the maximum pilot density over the template grid. For positive `d_max`, the range is `[(min_bandwidth + pilot_bandwidth) / 2, pilot_bandwidth]`, not `[min_bandwidth, pilot_bandwidth]`: high-density cells get bandwidths near the lower bound, sparse cells near the pilot bandwidth. Results depend on the template grid because `d_max` is taken over template cells.
